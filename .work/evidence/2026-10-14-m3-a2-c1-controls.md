## Methodological conclusion

**C1 is a completed negative *control experiment*.** The V0 embeddable positive reference and V2 non-embeddable negative reference both ended in the **same uninformative metadata-failure state**, on both predeclared client profiles. The earlier V1 r2 run #38155051380 also produced six INCONCLUSIVE outcomes with no direct media reads.

These observations weaken the hypothesis that **only the V1 candidate** explains the failure. They cannot distinguish among missing JavaScript/EJS runtime, reference extractor behavior, egress/network/environment constraints, changed provider behavior, or unsuitable source cases. The present no-JS/no-token environment was intentionally fixed and cannot be silently modified while calling the results directly comparable.

The original full 18-cell matrix is **not** complete: C1 observed only 4 planned cells, with 14 explicitly **MISSING**; V1 was **NOT_EVALUATED** in C1. No M3-A3 production architecture decision follows; `globalDecision=NOT_MADE_M3_A2`. Do not label any case `ABSENT`, `TOKEN_GATED` or `CHALLENGE_GATED` on this evidence.
